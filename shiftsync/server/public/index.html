Your schedule, perfectly in sync.

ShiftSync is a mobile scheduling app for students who work. It brings your classes, work shifts, events and tasks into one calendar, warns you when commitments collide, and helps you ask for a change when you need one.

Everything in one place

Add classes, shifts, events and tasks, or bring in events from your Google Calendar.

Conflicts caught early

ShiftSync flags overlapping commitments and gaps too short to travel, before they become a problem.

Easier conversations

Prepare a clear message to your manager or professor, then review and send it yourself.

Connecting Google is optional

You can use ShiftSync with a plain email account. If you choose to connect Google, ShiftSync reads your calendar so your real commitments are checked for conflicts, and adds events you create in ShiftSync. With Gmail connected, it can prepare a draft message and send it — but only the exact message you have read and confirmed.

Nothing is sent or changed without your confirmation. ShiftSync never emails anyone on your behalf automatically, and never deletes events from your Google Calendar.
