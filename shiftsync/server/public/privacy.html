Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

ShiftSync is a scheduling app operated by Zyrus Ponce. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what control you have. Questions go to zyrusponce02@gmail.com.

What ShiftSync collects

Data Why
Account — your name, email address and password To create and sign you into your account. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored only as a secure hash; we never see them.
Schedule — events, shifts, tasks, contacts you add, availability and preferences To show your calendar, detect conflicts and suggest new times.
Google Calendar — only if you connect it To read events on your primary calendar so your real commitments are checked for conflicts, and to add or update events for items you create in ShiftSync.
Gmail — only if you connect it To save a draft and send a message you have read and confirmed. ShiftSync cannot read your inbox or your existing mail.
Device — a session token and your notification settings To keep you signed in and to schedule the reminders you turn on. Both stay on your phone.

How your Google data is used

Connecting Google is optional, and you can disconnect at any time from Profile → Connected services. ShiftSync asks only for what it needs:

  • Calendar events — ShiftSync reads events within a window around today to check them for conflicts, and writes only events you create or edit in ShiftSync. It never deletes events from your Google Calendar.
  • Compose and send mail — ShiftSync prepares a draft about a scheduling conflict. Nothing is sent until you have read the message, seen the recipient, and confirmed.

ShiftSync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your Google data is used only to provide the features described above. It is never sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

AI features

If you turn on CalenBud, ShiftSync sends the relevant details of the commitments in question — their titles, dates and times — to an AI provider so it can word an explanation or a draft message. We use OpenRouter and Google Gemini for this. The content of your Google Calendar and Gmail is not sent beyond those details, and your Google access tokens and our API keys never leave our server. You can turn CalenBud off in Settings → AI Preferences, and ShiftSync falls back to fixed templates.

Where your data is kept

Your account and schedule are stored in a Supabase Postgres database, and the ShiftSync API runs on Render. Access to your rows is restricted to your own account. Google access tokens are encrypted before they are stored. A copy of your schedule is also kept on your phone so the app works offline.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not share your data with third parties, except the service providers named above who process it on our behalf.
  • We do not email anyone on your behalf without your confirmation.

Notifications

Reminders and alerts are scheduled on your own device; the content of a notification is not sent through any server. You can turn off each category in Settings → Notifications, or revoke notification permission in your phone's settings.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can edit or delete individual events and contacts in the app at any time, and disconnecting Google immediately stops all access to your calendar and mail. To delete your account and everything stored with it, email zyrusponce02@gmail.com from your account's address; we will delete it within 30 days and confirm when it is done.

Children

ShiftSync is not intended for children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date above. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
